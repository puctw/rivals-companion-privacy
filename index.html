Privacy Policy

Rivals Companion for Overwolf · Last updated 9 November 2026

In short: Rivals Companion has no servers and no accounts of its own. Your watchlist and settings stay on your PC. We don't use analytics, ads or tracking cookies. The only data that leaves your PC is what Overwolf and Tebex need to check or process a Premium subscription.

Who we are

Rivals Companion is an independent app made by puctw. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NetEase Games or Marvel. Contact: onunorodrigues@gmail.com.

Game data

During a Marvel Rivals match, the app receives lobby information (player names, UIDs and teams) from Overwolf's official Game Events feed. It uses this only to show the lobby on your screen. It doesn't save it, upload it or share it, except for players you add to your watchlist yourself.

Data stored on your PC

  • Watchlist: players you add, with the name, UID, tag and heroes you give them.
  • Settings: your app preferences, such as overlay opacity and alerts.
  • Backup file: if you use Save backup, a copy of your watchlist is written as a file to your Desktop.

This data is kept in the app's local storage inside Overwolf and is never sent to us. To delete it, remove players from your watchlist or uninstall the app, and delete any backup file you saved.

Premium subscription

If you subscribe to Premium, payment is handled by Tebex, which acts as the merchant of record. You enter your payment details on Tebex's checkout, never in the app, and we never see your card details.

  • To open checkout and to check whether your subscription is active, the app sends your Overwolf user ID and a temporary Overwolf session token to Overwolf's subscription service (subscriptions-api.overwolf.com).
  • Tebex gives us access to the records of your purchase (for example order ID, email address, country and subscription status). We use them only to provide support, handle refunds and meet tax and legal obligations.

Tebex's processing is covered by the Tebex Privacy Policy.

Overwolf

Rivals Companion runs on the Overwolf platform. Overwolf may collect usage and technical data under its own Privacy Policy, which we don't control. The app also checks Overwolf's public Game Events status page to tell you whether the feed is working. That request contains no personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you can ask to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, which is only the purchase records described above. Email us and we'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
